FAQ

A few things you might wonder before joining.

What is Slingshot?

AP prep you do on your own in 45 minutes a day: short video lessons, questions, written answers and review, in one planned session.

Do I still need an AP class or a tutor?

No. Slingshot teaches the whole course, start to finish, in about 50 hours.

How can 50 hours be enough?

A diagnostic at the start finds what you already know, and you skip it. Every session after that goes to the concepts you are missing or starting to forget.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

Students who start on time, answer 300 practice questions, master 80% of the skills and take a practice exam get a full refund if they score below a 4. Full terms come with enrollment.

How much does Slingshot cost?

A $500 refundable deposit reserves a spot. The full price is not announced yet.

Why are the lessons so short?

Working memory holds about four new ideas at a time. Each lesson teaches one concept in about a minute, then a question locks it in before the next one. We also measure how many ideas each sentence asks a student to hold, and rewrite the ones that ask too much.

Why are there so many questions?

Pulling an answer out of memory strengthens it far more than rereading does. Every lesson is followed by questions right away, and the same concepts come back the next day and later in the week.

What happens when I get a question wrong?

You go back to that lesson and try again. A concept counts as learned after five correct answers in a row.

Should I take notes?

You can skip them. Slingshot trains you to recall from memory, which is what the exam asks for, and it brings each concept back for review before it fades.

How are written answers graded?

Against the College Board rubric for that question type, with feedback on what earned points and what is missing. Then you try again. Writing practice runs through the whole course, because cramming it into the last weeks works poorly.

What results is this based on?

In May 2026, 15 students prepared for their AP exams with it. All 15 scored a 4 or 5, with an average of 4.73. The lesson design was reviewed with learning scientists and experienced AP teachers.

How does the $1,000 work?

Students earn it in cash or rewards as they complete the course. Full terms come with enrollment.

Which AP subjects are covered?

English Language, English Literature, US History, World History, US Government, Human Geography, Psychology, Environmental Science and Microeconomics.
